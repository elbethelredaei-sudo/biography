Introduction

My name is Elbethel Redaei, and I am currently a Grade 9 STEM student. I live with my parents in Bahir Dar, the capital city of the Amhara Region. I was born on 20 May 2003, and I have always been passionate about learning, growing, and achieving my goals.

I started my education at kindergarten, where I completed three years of learning. After that, I joined SOS Herman Geman Primary School, where I spent the last eight years studying and enjoying school life. I worked hard, performed well in my studies, and also did my best in the regional exams.

After completing my primary school studies, I sat for the entrance exam of the STEM center and was admitted. I am still learning and growing every day, and I remain committed to my education.

Family

My family consists of four people: me, my younger brother, my father, and my mother. We are a happy and close family, and we support each other in every step of our lives. My brother is a kind, funny, and respectful person, and I love him very much. My family is a source of encouragement and happiness for me.

My Future Goals

After finishing secondary school, I want to continue my education in a country that provides quality education and scholarships. My dream career is to become an engineer and to build a successful future through hard work, discipline, and determination.
